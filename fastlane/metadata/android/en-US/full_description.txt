<p>An Xposed module for cleaning up Android's Share Sheet. Added settings app, Quick Share filtering, and delete-after-sharing for screenshots.</p>

<p><b>Features:</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Hide Direct Share suggestions and block share target profiling</li>
<li>Hide Quick Share / Nearby Share</li>
<li>Option to delete shared screenshots after sharing (root required)</li>
<li>Built-in settings app</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Requirements:</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Android 11 or higher and AOSP rom</li>
<li>LSPosed framework</li>
</ul>
